## The one failure (`c04`): the harness expected the wrong thing

`c04` passed its complete pre-reboot verdict (`complete_verdict`/`pre_reboot_verdict` `passed`), its pair verdict before the reboot, and all four zone lifecycle steps; management was disabled and both guests rebooted (secondary first). The resumed controller then failed only on (`result.json` `reboot_after_recovery.failures`, verbatim):

```
after reboot: pair: the primary's udp catalog serial [1790723604] differs from the state receipt's 1790723542
after reboot: pair: the primary's tcp catalog serial [1790723604] differs from the state receipt's 1790723542
```

**Harness, not product.** After the reboot the controller repeats `check_fresh_primary_pair` (`deploy/e2e/dns-kill-matrix/run_cell.py:9945-9950` at `e3591875`), which requires the served catalog SOA serial to equal the DNS state receipt's `primary_catalog_serial` (`run_cell.py:12190-12202`; README "the served catalog serial equals the state receipt's", `README.md:1019`, "The same check runs after a reboot", `README.md:1042`, and step 5 "the pair as above", `README.md:1071`). The state receipt records the serial of the switch (1790723542); the zone lifecycle ran between the pre-reboot pair check and the reboot, and every catalog membership change raises the producer serial: the Agent's add publication wrote 1790723543, the daemon re-stamped to 1790723602, delete wrote 1790723603 and re-add 1790723604 (below). The product policy admits exactly this: "Zone publication follows a daemon re-stamp during a propagation wave only when the serial is strictly higher …" (`docs/DNS-ENGINE-ARTIFACT.md:1749-1752`), and the Agent treats the receipt's serial as a floor, refusing only a producer serial that "predates its active state" (`cmd/agent/dns_engine_pdns.go:565-567`, same at `:963-965` and `cmd/agent/dns_engine_pdns_catalog.go:1239`). Everything else after the reboot held: both servers answered catalog 1790723604 identically over UDP and TCP (no re-stamp at the reboot), the member SOA 2026083101 and `www` A 192.0.2.10 on both, the re-added child `s2.s1-kill.test` SOA 2026092803 and `www.s2` A 192.0.2.10 on both (`fresh_primary_child.failures` empty), management units inactive/disabled, 31/31 DNS-only samples, and the host's `peer-verdict-after-reboot.json` `status: passed` with both catalogs listing member and child (its `combined_exit` is 1 only because the guest exited 1). This is not a setup problem that could be worked around without changing a pass rule, so it was not worked around; the cell's guests were stopped with overlays kept and the run continued. It was not re-run.
